Lightspeed Voice

Top pick
Insurance-focused cloud phone system, family owned since 2009, based in Florida. Not connected to Salesforce (Vertafore partner page).
Pros
  • AgencyZoom: caller pop-up, calls and missed calls logged after hang-up, recordings, two-way texting, AI call summaries (support matrix, integration page). Set up by Lightspeed's team (AgencyZoom)
  • Also connects to EZLynx, HawkSoft, Applied Epic, AMS360 and QQCatalyst (Lightspeed)
  • On GOAL's integration list
  • Supports Yealink, Poly and Grandstream desk phones, plus desktop and mobile apps
Cons
  • No published pricing; quote required (pricing page)
  • Terms auto-renew for the same length; cancellation accepted only 120 to 60 days before renewal; charges cannot be cancelled mid-term; annual increase of 3% or inflation, whichever is higher (terms of service). Two Yelp reviewers report 3-year terms
  • Yelp 1.5 from 10 reviews, citing dropped calls and contract lock-in (Yelp mirror); Google reported at 4.9 from about 966 reviews. No G2 listing
  • 3.5% fee on card payments; 50% of monthly charges due if service is not live within 90 days of signing (terms)

RingCentral RingEX

Runner-up
General business phone system. Not insurance-specific. Not on GOAL's integration list.
Pros
  • Published prices; 30 days' notice to stop a renewal (terms)
  • AI call transcription and summaries included on every plan (pricing)
  • Porting 5 to 10 business days typical, 10 to 15 from smaller carriers (RingCentral)
  • Francis already knows the platform
  • G2 4.2 from 1,486 reviews (G2); Capterra 4.2 from 1,206 (Capterra)
Cons
  • AgencyZoom's built-in connection covers texting and click-to-call (AgencyZoom). Call logging, recordings on the client record and caller pop-ups need a paid connector (App Connect)
  • Click-to-call from AgencyZoom requires AgencyZoom Pro (AgencyZoom)
  • Advanced is the minimum plan: Core lacks automatic recording and CRM integrations
  • Reviewers report unexpected auto-renewals, extra-cost texting add-ons and a hard-to-navigate admin portal

06

Open questions

Answers that could change the pick

1
Will Lightspeed offer a 12-month term with the per-user price in writing?
If only a 3-year auto-renewing term is offered, RingCentral becomes the pick.
2
Which AgencyZoom plan is the agency on?
RingCentral click-to-call requires Pro. Moving up from Growth adds $120 to $150 a month. Ask Lightspeed whether its click-to-dial works without Pro.
3
How many people need a phone line, and how many numbers will port?
Pricing assumes 8 users and about 10 numbers.
4
Desk phones or apps only?
NEC Univerge handsets are unlikely to work on a new system, so desk phones may be a new cost on either platform.
5
Is an agency management system used for the independent book?
Lightspeed connects to EZLynx, HawkSoft, Applied Epic, AMS360 and QQCatalyst. RingCentral would need separate work.
6
How many texts does the agency send a month?
RingCentral Advanced includes 100 per user per month. Lightspeed charges $0.015 per text.
7
Do producers want a power dialer for outbound follow-up?
Lightspeed includes an optional dialer (Automate). RingCentral's dialer is a separate product.
8
Does text history need to live in the new system?
Francis expects moving text history to be unlikely on any platform. Plan on an archive.
